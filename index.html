A HALLOWEEN EXPERIENCE
FOR THE LITTLE ONES.

Haunted Fest Halloween Edition is a fun-filled Halloween experience specially designed with kids and families in mind. It's a day where children can dress up, play, explore, make memories and enjoy an exciting Halloween atmosphere.

From creative activities and fun games to swimming, photography, fashion and exciting surprises, there's something for every child to enjoy.
